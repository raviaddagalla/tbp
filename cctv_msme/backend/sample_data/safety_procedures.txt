SAFETY PROCEDURES - MSME STEEL FABRICATION UNIT

1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
Every worker entering the production floor must wear a helmet, safety
goggles, and steel-toe boots. Helmets must be worn in the loading dock,
machine zone, and storage area at all times. Workers without a helmet
are stopped at the gate and reminded before entering.

2. Loading Dock Operations
Forklifts in the loading dock must keep a 2-metre clearance from
pedestrian walkways. Loading bay doors close fully during high winds.
Hand pallet jacks are stored against the east wall after use. Spills of
oil or coolant are cleaned immediately and reported to the shift
supervisor with a yellow hazard cone placed within 5 minutes.

3. Machine Zone Rules
CNC lathe operators must not reach across rotating spindles. Guards are
to remain in place except during tool changes. Only trained operators
may run the press brake. Ear protection is mandatory when any machine
is cutting steel. Emergency stop buttons are tested every Monday
morning before the first shift.

4. Storage Area
Steel bundles are stacked no higher than 1.5 metres. Aisles must stay
clear for the fire extinguisher access. Full containers are sealed and
labelled with the batch number. Inventory is counted every Friday by
the warehouse lead.

5. Emergency Response
Fire exits are marked in green. Assembly point is the north parking
lot. First aid kit is at the supervisor office. In case of smoke or
fire, raise the alarm, move away from the source, and report to the
floor supervisor immediately.

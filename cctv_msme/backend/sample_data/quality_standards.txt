PRODUCTION QUALITY STANDARDS - SHIFT SUMMARY GUIDE

Batch Acceptance Criteria
Steel sheets must measure within plus or minus 0.5 millimetre of the
order specification. Surface defects such as rust pitting deeper than
0.2 mm cause rejection. Every batch carries a unique batch number
stamped on the bundle tag.

Inspection Points
1. Visual check at the cutting station - look for burrs and edge cracks.
2. Dimensional check at the drilling station - every 50th piece is
   measured against the drawing.
3. Weld inspection at the welding bay - fillet welds are checked for
   porosity and undercut before the part moves to painting.

Defect Codes
D1 - wrong dimension, D2 - surface rust, D3 - burr, D4 - weld porosity,
D5 - paint adhesion failure. The inspector records the defect code and
the machine that produced the part in the daily log.

Shift Handover
The outgoing shift records production count, rejected pieces, machine
downtime, and pending quality checks in the handover register. The
incoming shift supervisor signs the register before starting work.

Downtime Reporting
Any machine stoppage longer than 15 minutes is reported to the floor
supervisor with the reason code: material shortage, tool change,
maintenance, or power failure. Downtime is summarised in the weekly
efficiency report.
